# TabPulse Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026

**TabPulse** is 100% local-first. We do **not** collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share your browsing history, tab URLs, page titles, or any personal data.

## Local-Only Processing
- **Zero Telemetry & Zero Network Calls**: TabPulse performs all semantic tab classification, duplicate URL normalization, RAM tab sleeping (`chrome.tabs.discard`), Focus Mode, and Workspace Session storage exclusively inside your local Chrome browser.
- **Local Storage (`chrome.storage.local`)**: Used solely to persist user preferences, custom rules, the 1-click Undo snapshot, and user-saved Workspace Sessions on your device.
