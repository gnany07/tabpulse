⚡ TabPulse — Privacy Policy

100% Local-First • Zero Data Collection • Zero Telemetry

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Short Version: TabPulse runs 100% locally inside your browser. We do not collect, transmit, store remotely, sell, or share your browsing history, tab URLs, page titles, or any personal information.

1. Data Collection & Network Activity

TabPulse makes zero external network requests (no analytics, no crash reporters, no remote APIs, no ads, and no tracking scripts). All tab classification, duplicate URL detection, RAM sleeping, and workspace snapshots execute entirely on your device.

2. Local Storage Usage

TabPulse uses Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local) strictly to persist:

  • Your extension preferences (Auto Mode state, grouping strategy, minimum group threshold, Auto-Sleep timer, and duplicate matching filters).
  • Custom grouping rules you create.
  • Your temporary 1-click Undo snapshot and named Workspace Sessions you explicitly save.

All locally stored data remains strictly on your machine and can be cleared at any time by deleting workspaces or uninstalling the extension.

3. Chrome Permissions Justification

  • tabs: Required to read open tab URLs/titles locally for grouping, duplicate detection, cross-window search, and native chrome.tabs.discard() RAM sleeping.
  • tabGroups: Required to create, update, color, collapse, and expand native Chrome Tab Groups.
  • storage: Required to save your settings, custom rules, undo snapshot, and saved Workspace Sessions locally.
  • sidePanel: Required to provide the optional persistent Chrome Side Panel view.
  • alarms: Required to run the optional periodic idle-tab RAM Sleeper check in Manifest V3.

4. Contact
